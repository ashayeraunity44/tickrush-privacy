TickRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026  |  Applies to the Android game “TickRush” (package com.ahmadashayer.tickrush) in every version, with or without ads

The short version. TickRush is a free multiplayer timing game. It does not ask you to create an account and does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or precise location. It has no chat and no in-app purchases. To let you play online it uses services from Unity and Google, which handle a player name and an anonymous player ID. The game is free and is supported by ads: versions that show ads use Google AdMob, which may use your device’s advertising ID (see section 4). We do not run our own servers and do not keep a database of players.

1. Who we are

TickRush is made by Ahmad Ashayer (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. You can contact us about this policy or your information at: ashayeraunity44@gmail.com.

For the information described in this policy we decide why and how it is used, so we are the “controller” under data-protection laws such as the GDPR. Unity Technologies and Google act for us as service providers for the services listed in section 5; for advertising, Google AdMob also decides for itself how it uses advertising data (see section 4).

2. What information the game uses

To run the game for everyone in the match. Passed between the phones in the match through Unity’s Relay service, over an encrypted connection. We do not store match data after the match ends.

Your ad choices (only where the game asks for consent). Whether you accepted or refused personalised ads. To respect your choice. Stored on your phone by Google’s consent tool, and passed to AdMob.
Settings stored on your phone. Volume levels, left-handed mode, the generated name, and a note about your last online match so you can come back to it. So the game remembers your choices. Only on your phone. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the game removes it.

3. What the game does not do

  • It does not use your microphone or camera, and has no voice or text chat.
  • It does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, phone number or e-mail address.
  • It has no in-app purchases and does not ask for payment details.
  • It does not sell your information to anyone for money, and we do not use it ourselves for profiling.

4. Ads

TickRush is free and may show ads. Versions that show ads use Google AdMob. This section applies whenever the version you are using shows ads. A version without ads does not collect the advertising information in the table above.

What AdMob may collect and use

To choose, show and measure ads, AdMob and the ad networks it works with may use the advertising ID of your device (a resettable ID that is not linked to your name), your IP address, device and operating-system information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. They may use device storage and similar technologies for this. Their use of this information is described in how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google’s Privacy Policy. AdMob’s own rules are at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom and other regions where the law requires it). Before ads are shown the game asks for your consent using Google’s consent message. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later using the privacy options in the game’s Settings.
  • Reset or remove the advertising ID. In Android: Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads — you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Ads will still appear, but they will not be personalised.
  • Google’s ad settings. You can manage personalised ads for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.

Children

Ads are not meant for children. The game is not directed to children under 13 (see section 9).

5. Services we use

TickRush relies on these providers. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer Sessions and Relay) by Unity Technologies — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play by Google — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (only in versions with ads) by Google — policies.google.com/privacy

Online match traffic between your phone and the match server is sent over an encrypted connection (DTLS).

6. Why we use information (legal bases)

  • To run the game you asked to play (names, anonymous ID, match information, network information): needed to provide the service (performance of a contract).
  • To show ads that are personalised in regions where consent is required: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time (section 4).
  • To show non-personalised ads, limit repeated ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure: our legitimate interest in running a free, safe game.

7. Who receives information, and transfers abroad

Information goes only to the providers named in section 5 and, during a match, to the other players in that match (they see your player name and what you do in the game). We do not sell information. Unity and Google run services in many countries, so information can be processed outside your own country, including outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom. They protect it with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses and, where available, data-privacy frameworks; see their policies for details.

8. How long information is kept

We do not run our own servers, so we do not hold a database of players. Information that is needed for a match is kept by Unity’s services only while the match exists and as described in Unity’s privacy policy. Advertising information is kept by Google as described in Google’s privacy policy. Information stored on your phone stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.

9. Children

TickRush is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given information to the game, contact us and we will help get it removed.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Sign in or not. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. Without it the game uses a generated name.
  • Delete local data. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game.
  • Your Google Play Games profile (including its name) is managed in your Google account and in the Google Play Games app.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Brazil or California) you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict some uses, to take it with you, and to withdraw consent. Write to ashayeraunity44@gmail.com. Because the game has no account system, we can only act on information we can identify; for information that Unity or Google hold, we will tell you how to contact them. You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.
  • California. We do not sell personal information for money. Showing ads with the advertising ID may count as “sharing” for cross-context advertising under California law; you can opt out by resetting the advertising ID or turning off ads personalisation in Android settings (section 4), or by writing to us.

11. Security

Online connections use encryption. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but the game keeps the information it handles to the minimum needed to play.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the new version is released.
