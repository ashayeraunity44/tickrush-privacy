TickRush — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026  |  Applies to the Android app “TickRush” (package com.ahmadashayer.tickrush)

The short version. TickRush is a multiplayer timing game. It does not ask you to create an account, does not use your microphone, camera, contacts, photos or precise location, has no chat, shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. To let you play online it uses services from Unity and Google, which handle a player name and an anonymous player ID. We (the developer) do not run our own servers and do not keep a database of players.

1. Who we are

TickRush is made by Ahmad Ashayer (“we”, “us”), an independent developer. You can contact us about this policy or your data at: ashayeraunity44@gmail.com.

2. What information the game uses

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it goes
Player name. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your Google Play Games display name. If you do not sign in, a random name that the game generates on your phone (for example “Swift Fox 42”). You cannot type a name. To show who is who during a match, on the scoreboard and in the list of open matches (a match is named after its host, for example “Swift Fox 42’s Match”). Sent to the other players in your match and to Unity’s multiplayer service while the match exists.
Anonymous player ID. A random ID that Unity Authentication gives to this installation. It is not linked to your name, e-mail address or Google account. To create and join online matches, and to give you your seat back if your connection drops or the app is closed during a match. Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies).
Match information. The match name, which games and how many rounds were chosen, the region of the match server, and what happens in the match (scores, button timings, answers). To run the game for everyone in the match. Passed between the phones in the match through Unity’s Relay service. Match data is not stored by us after the match ends.
Network information. Your IP address and connection quality (ping). To connect your phone to the match server and to show a ping value in the list of matches. Handled by Unity’s services to route the connection. We do not collect or store it ourselves.
Google Play Games sign-in (optional). If you tap SIGN IN, Google tells the game your Play Games display name and that you are signed in. To use your Google Play Games name as your player name. Google. The game uses only the display name; it does not read your e-mail address or contacts.
Settings stored on your phone. Volume levels, left-handed mode, the generated name, and a note about your last online match so you can come back to it. So the game remembers your choices. Only on your phone. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the game removes it.

3. What the game does not do

  • It does not use your microphone or camera, and has no voice or text chat.
  • It does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files, phone number or e-mail address.
  • It does not show ads, does not read the advertising ID, and has no in-app purchases in this version.
  • It does not sell your information and does not use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Services we use

TickRush relies on these providers, which process the information above for us as service providers. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Multiplayer Sessions and Relay) by Unity Technologies — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play Games Services and Google Play by Google — policies.google.com/privacy

Online match traffic between your phone and the match server is sent over an encrypted connection (DTLS).

5. How long information is kept

We do not run our own servers, so we do not hold a database of players. Information that is needed for a match is kept by Unity’s services only while the match exists and as described in Unity’s privacy policy. Information stored on your phone stays there until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Sign in or not. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. Without it the game uses a generated name.
  • Delete local data. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game.
  • Your Google Play Games profile (including its name) is managed in your Google account and in the Google Play Games app.
  • Access, correction, deletion, objection. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California) you may have the right to ask what information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Write to ashayeraunity44@gmail.com. Because the game has no account system, we can only act on information we can identify; for information that Unity or Google hold, we will tell you how to contact them.

7. Children

TickRush is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given information to the game, contact us and we will help get it removed.

8. Security

Online connections use encryption. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but the game keeps the information it handles to the minimum needed to play.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your information — for example if ads are added — we will update this page and the “Last updated” date before the new version is released.
